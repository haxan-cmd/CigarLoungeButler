Finish a match and drop your end-of-game screenshot in the submissions channel.

2

3

The monthly cycle & titles

🗓️ Each month

A new bounty and season open together and run about a month. Clear the bounty's weapon objectives to climb your player title. When the month ends, the season champions are enshrined in the Hall of Fame and the season titles reset — weapon ranks, marks and all-time titles carry over forever.

Player ranks

Earned by completing bounties: Lounger → Insider → Regular → Made Man → High Roller → Kingpin → Legend.

🏆 All-time titles

  • Grand Marshal — most boards held overall
  • Weapons Master — most weapon boards
  • Campaign Master — most map boards

Season titles (reset each month)

  • Apex · best average kills
  • Frenzied · best average takedowns
  • Most Dominant · best blend of Kill Share & Warlord

What is this?

The Cigar Lounge is a Discord community for competitive Chivalry 2. Every scorecard players post is parsed and logged, feeding live weapon and map records, monthly bounties, seasonal titles, and a per-player registry card. These pages are the public window into all of it, drawn live from the same records the community sees in Discord.
